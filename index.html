Welcome to hypnos

      
Standard polysomnography metrics from an EDF file, computed entirely in your browser.

      

        
        Private by design. Your recording is processed on this device and is never uploaded. There is no account and no server holding your data.
      

    


    

      
How it works

      

        
1Pick an EDF file. Only the header and the EEG, EOG and EMG channels are read, so a night-long file of several hundred MB is fine.

        
2R runs in your browser. mrpheus stages each 30 s epoch and hypnoR computes the sleep summary, using R compiled to WebAssembly.

        
3Explore and export. Zoom the plots, then download the markers or the hypnogram as CSV.

      

    


    

      
What you get

      

        
Stage composition across the night

        
Hypnogram with hover and zoom

        
Spectrogram of the staging EEG channel, 0 to 30 Hz, with its time axis linked to the hypnogram

        
Clinical markers: total sleep time, efficiency, sleep onset latency, wake after sleep onset, REM and N3 latency, and stage percentages

      

    


    

      
Before you start

      

        
First load. The first visit downloads R and its packages, which can take a minute or two. You can read this while it loads.

        
Automatic scoring. Stages come from an automatic model (YASA) applied to one EEG channel, a central derivation when the file has one, with EOG and EMG where present. Stretches where the EEG is flat are not staged and are left out of the markers. You can change the channels and the analysis window under Analysis settings. Check the stages against a scored hypnogram before relying on them.

        
Files. EDF and EDF+ recordings that are continuous.
